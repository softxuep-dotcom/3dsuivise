Desert Survivor

A crash left you in the open sand. Find water and shade by day, hold the fire against the dogs by night.

1Cut cactus, draw from wells, hunt dogs for hide 2Stock deadwood before dusk, feed the fire, seal the gate 3Take a weapon line — sabre or sword — to tier 3, then draw out the alpha

Water or food hitting zero kills you outright. The daytime sun drives your warmth up into heatstroke; the night cold drags it down into hypothermia — neither kills, but either leaves you barely able to move. Gathering and swinging both cost stamina, and only standing still restores it quickly.
