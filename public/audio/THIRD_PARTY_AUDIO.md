# Third-party audio

All shipped third-party audio is permitted for commercial use. The short files in
`audio/sfx/` were converted to MP3 and, where noted, trimmed for this game.

## Kenney — Creative Commons CC0

- [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio): footsteps, weapon swings, cloth and pickup foley.
- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds): body, stone, wood, plank and metal impacts.
- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds): confirmation and warning cues.
